Generate one supporting design and pair it with the existing security architecture diagram. Their recorded digests bind the two upload files, and the visible model fingerprint detects a stale embedded diagram.

  • Define the review scope, exhaustive flow/objective boundary, and data assumptions.
  • Record each control beside its mitigation trigger and expected verification.
  • Keep publication blocked until the defense is verified; human referral does not close an open control.

Private-decision retrieval and exact artifact comparison are not implemented. Repository paths preserve traceability from every claim to its evidence, and the ignored archive contains exactly the two upload files.
