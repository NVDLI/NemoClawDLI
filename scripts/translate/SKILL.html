Same-branch localization

Begin at the target locale node. Review English and localized prose together. Keep runtime and machine contracts canonical, but keep learner-facing prompts, logs, errors, and comments localized. When a locale pins a style-PIC reference, preserve that blob exactly and carry its decisions across the locale. Only a style-PIC-authored follow-up may update the origin commit and pinned digest.

Use data-localization-scope="en-shell" only for an English-only presentation wrapper around unchanged translated prose. Mark its inline control data-localization-scope="en". The digest still invalidates when body prose changes.
