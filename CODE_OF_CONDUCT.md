# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Keep project spaces useful, respectful, and safe. Discuss the work, not the person. Welcome
questions from learners and contributors with different levels of experience. Give actionable
feedback, credit sources and collaborators, and protect private or security-sensitive information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, deliberate
misinformation, and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository Issues, Discussions, pull requests, reviews, release channels,
and project events. It also applies when someone represents the project in another public space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not publish a sensitive conduct report in an Issue or Discussion. On GitHub, use the platform's
[private content-reporting flow](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for abusive content. Security vulnerabilities follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, lock threads, reject contributions,
or ban participants when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement should be proportionate,
documented privately where needed, and applied without regard to contributor status. A maintainer
named in a report must not decide its outcome.

Community intake remains disabled while external publication approval is pending. Before enabling
it, the release owner must verify that the public host's private reporting and moderation controls
work as documented in [`docs/release_playbook.md`](docs/release_playbook.md).
